BlinkyBits

BlinkyBits is the independent engineering consultancy of Ireneusz Guzik, specializing in embedded firmware, low-power wireless technologies, and connected devices.

With 14+ years of engineering experience across semiconductor companies and technology startups, I help companies develop reliable, energy-efficient embedded solutions — from system architecture and technical decisions to hands-on firmware implementation.

My expertise covers BLE, UWB, cellular connectivity, sensor integration, and medical wearables. I work on both new firmware platforms and complex existing systems, taking ownership of technical challenges throughout product development.

Beyond firmware development, I focus on hardware debugging, power profiling, and optimization, bringing a practical, system-level perspective to embedded engineering.

My experience spans early development through commercial deployment, including long-term collaboration on medical and wireless products.
