BlinkyBits

BlinkyBits is the independent engineering consultancy of Ireneusz Guzik, a Staff / Principal Firmware Engineer specializing in embedded systems, wireless technologies, and medical wearables.

With 14+ years of experience, including roles at Nordic Semiconductor, Silicon Labs, Estimote, and Wearlinq, I help companies develop reliable, energy-efficient connected products — from initial architecture and hardware bring-up to commercial production.

My expertise includes Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), Nordic SoCs, nRF Connect SDK, Zephyr RTOS, UWB, cellular connectivity, and sensor integration.

I combine technical leadership with hands-on firmware development, hardware debugging, power profiling, and ultra-low-power optimization.

Based in Poland, I work with international clients through remote B2B engagements, offering long-term engineering collaboration and specialized technical consulting, with occasional on-site support when needed.
